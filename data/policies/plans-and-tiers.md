# Plans and Subscription Tiers

Fernway Workspace is offered on three subscription tiers: Free, Pro and Enterprise. A workspace has exactly one tier at a time. The tier decides features, storage, support response times and refund limits (see the Refund Policy).

## Free tier

Free costs $0 and is meant for individuals and very small teams.

- Up to 3 members per workspace.
- 2 GB of pooled storage; maximum file size 100 MB.
- 7-day version history.
- Integrations: Slack only.
- Sign-in with email and password or Google sign-in. No SAML single sign-on (SSO).
- Support: community forum and email, best effort, target first response within 3 business days.
- Optional add-on: the Storage Pack (+50 GB) costs $12 per month and is the only paid purchase available on Free.

## Pro tier

Pro costs $29 per member per month, or $290 per member per year when billed annually.

- Up to 100 members per workspace.
- 1 TB of pooled storage; maximum file size 5 GB.
- 180-day version history.
- All integrations, including Slack, GitHub, Jira and outgoing webhooks.
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) enforcement for all members. Google sign-in is available; SAML SSO is not included.
- Support: email and live chat, Monday to Friday, first response within 1 business day.

## Enterprise tier

Enterprise is sold on an annual contract with custom pricing and a minimum of 25 seats.

- Unlimited members and storage; maximum file size 5 GB.
- Unlimited version history and an exportable audit log.
- SAML single sign-on (SSO) and SCIM user provisioning.
- Choice of data residency region (US or EU).
- 99.9% monthly uptime commitment with service credits (see the Refund Policy).
- A named Customer Success Manager (CSM).
- Priority support 24/7. First response targets: P1 (critical) within 1 hour, P2 (high) within 4 business hours, P3 (normal) within 1 business day.

## Comparing support response times

| Tier | Channels | First response target |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Community forum, email | 3 business days (best effort) |
| Pro | Email, live chat | 1 business day |
| Enterprise | Email, chat, phone, CSM | P1 1 hour, P2 4 business hours, P3 1 business day |

Response targets are for first response, not resolution.
