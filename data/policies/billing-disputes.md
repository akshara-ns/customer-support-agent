# Billing Disputes

## Raising a dispute

Customers must raise a billing dispute within 90 days of the charge. Disputes older than 90 days are not reviewed. Identity verification is required before reviewing any invoice.

## Duplicate or incorrect charges

1. Find the invoice and confirm the charge is a duplicate or was billed at the wrong amount.
2. If the amount to correct is within the agent's refund limit for the customer's tier and the contract is active, issue a refund against the duplicate invoice.
3. Otherwise, create a billing escalation with priority P2, including both invoice numbers.

## Charges after a contract ended

A charge dated after the contract end date is a billing error. Do not refund it directly. Escalate to the billing team with priority P2 (see Contract Status).

## Chargebacks

If the customer has already filed a chargeback with their bank or card issuer for a charge, support must not issue a refund for that same charge, because the customer would be refunded twice. Create a billing escalation with priority P2 and tell the customer the bank process will decide the outcome.

## Invoices and tax details

- Pro customers can download invoices and add a VAT or tax ID under Settings > Billing. Tax ID changes apply to future invoices only.
- Enterprise customers request invoice changes through their Customer Success Manager.
- Support cannot reissue an invoice that has already been paid with different amounts.
