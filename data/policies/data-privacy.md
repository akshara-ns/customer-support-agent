# Data Privacy and Retention

## Data export

Workspace owners and admins can export all workspace data at any time as JSON and Markdown under Settings > Data > Export. Expired workspaces can export during their 30-day read-only period.

## Retention and deletion

- Closed workspaces: data is deleted 30 days after closure.
- Expired contracts: data is deleted 90 days after the contract end date.
- After deletion, encrypted backups are purged within a further 35 days.
- Deleted data cannot be restored by support.

## Privacy requests (GDPR and CCPA)

Requests to access, correct or delete personal data are handled by the privacy team within 30 days. Support agents must not process these requests themselves; create an escalation ticket with priority P2 and the customer's request in their own words.

## Data residency

Enterprise customers choose a US or EU data region when the contract starts. Changing the region later requires a migration arranged by the Customer Success Manager. Free and Pro workspaces are hosted in the US region.

## Security

Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or later.

## Confidentiality in support

Support agents never share information about one account with anyone who has not passed identity verification for that account, and never confirm whether an email address has a Fernway account.
