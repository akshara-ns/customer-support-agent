# Account Changes and Identity Verification

## Identity verification

Before discussing account details or making any change, support must verify the customer's identity with two factors: the email address on file and the 6-digit support PIN shown under Settings > Security. If either does not match, do not discuss the account; offer to help with general questions only. After three failed attempts in one conversation, stop and create an escalation ticket for the security team.

## Changing the billing email or contact

Only a workspace owner can change the billing email. After identity verification, a support agent may update the billing email on the owner's behalf. A confirmation is sent to both the old and the new address.

## Upgrading a plan

Upgrades take effect immediately. The customer is charged a prorated amount for the rest of the current billing period. Free to Pro is self-serve; any change to Enterprise is handled by the sales team.

## Downgrading a plan

Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. No refund is given for the current period. A workspace can move from Pro to Free only if it has 3 members or fewer and uses no more than 2 GB of storage.

## Adding or removing seats

- Pro: owners and admins add or remove seats themselves under Settings > Billing. Added seats are charged prorated; removed seats are credited on the next invoice.
- Enterprise: seat changes require a contract amendment through the customer's Customer Success Manager.

## Transferring workspace ownership

Ownership can only be transferred by the current owner. Support may start the transfer, but it completes only after the current owner confirms by email within 7 days.

## Closing an account

Only the owner can close a workspace. Support should offer a data export first. A closed workspace can be restored within 30 days; after that its data is deleted (see Data Privacy).
