## What is the Fernway Partner Program?

Agencies and consultants who resell Fernway to their clients can join the Partner Program. Partners get a reseller dashboard, a 15% margin on Pro subscriptions they manage, and early access to new features.

## How do partners get paid?

Partner margins are paid monthly by bank transfer for the previous month's paid invoices.

## Can a partner manage billing for a client?

Yes. A partner can be added as a billing contact on a client's Pro workspace. The client remains the workspace owner.
