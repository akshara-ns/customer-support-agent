# Refund Policy

This policy sets how much a support agent may refund, and within what time window, for each subscription tier. Amounts above an agent's limit are never refunded directly; they are escalated to the billing team.

## Conditions that apply to every refund

- The customer's identity must be verified first (email on file plus the 6-digit support PIN; see Account Changes).
- The contract status must be active. Expired and suspended accounts are not eligible for agent-issued refunds (see Contract Status).
- One refund per invoice. If an invoice has already been refunded, fully or partially, no further refund may be issued on that invoice. If the customer disputes the earlier refund, create a billing escalation.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days to appear.
- Refunds are issued against a specific invoice and may not exceed the amount of that invoice.

## Free tier refunds

<!-- tiers: free -->

The only paid purchase on Free is the Storage Pack add-on ($12 per month).

- Window: within 7 days of the charge.
- Agent limit: up to $20 per request.
- Free workspaces have no subscription fee, so there is nothing else to refund.

## Pro tier refunds

<!-- tiers: pro -->

- Window: within 30 days of the charge.
- Agent limit: up to $200 per request.
- Annual Pro plans can be refunded on a prorated basis within 30 days of purchase or renewal, still subject to the $200 agent limit. Larger annual refunds are escalated to the billing team.
- Requests above $200, or older than 30 days, must be escalated to the billing team with priority P2.

## Enterprise tier refunds and service credits

<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

- Window: within 60 days of the invoice date.
- Agent limit: up to $1,000 per request, as a refund or a service credit.
- Anything above $1,000 is escalated to the customer's Customer Success Manager and the finance team.
- Uptime service credits: if monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, the customer receives a credit of 10% of that month's fee; below 99.0%, 25%. Service credits are applied to the next invoice and are not paid out as cash.

## What is not refundable

- Months of service that have already ended, except where a billing error is confirmed.
- The remainder of the current billing period after a downgrade. Downgrades take effect at the end of the period instead.
- Charges for which the customer has already filed a chargeback with their bank (see Billing Disputes).
