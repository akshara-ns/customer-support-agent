# Contract Status Rules

Every paid workspace has a contract status: active, expired or suspended. Free workspaces are always treated as active. The status decides what the customer and support agents may do.

## Active contracts

An active contract allows every action available to the customer's tier: refunds within the tier's limits, plan and seat changes, and account updates.

## Expired contracts

A contract becomes expired when its end date passes without renewal.

- The workspace becomes read-only for 30 days after the contract end date. Members can view and export data but cannot edit.
- Allowed actions: renewal, data export, and updating the billing contact for renewal.
- Not allowed: refunds, plan changes other than renewal, and adding seats.
- Data is deleted 90 days after the contract end date (see Data Privacy).
- A charge billed after the contract end date is treated as a billing error. Support agents must not refund it directly; create a billing escalation with priority P2 so the billing team can reverse it.

## Suspended accounts

An account is suspended after a payment fails three times over 14 days, or when the Trust and Safety team finds a Terms of Service violation.

- Members cannot sign in. Workspace owners can still reach the billing page to update the payment method.
- Support agents may not issue refunds or make account changes on a suspended account.
- The only action available to support is to create an escalation ticket: to the billing team for payment suspensions, or to Trust and Safety for policy suspensions.
- Never share details of a Trust and Safety investigation with the customer. Tell them the case has been passed to the responsible team and give the ticket number.
- A payment suspension is lifted automatically within 1 hour of a successful payment.
